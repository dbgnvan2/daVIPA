## TST-A2, filed and closed on the same day

Filed as adjacent and deferred ("that is LRP-M11's criterion, and this batch is
the red suite"), then closed on the review pass, because the guard turned out
to be two lines in tests that already held the right fixtures.

The measurement that made it worth filing: with `ToolbarWrap.fit`'s threshold
reverted from `min_one_row_px()` to `one_row_px()`, **all 131 tests** in
`tests/test_list_screen_resize.py` and `tests/test_screen_resize_spc.py` stayed
green. The two near-misses both just missed — one asserts `fit(one_row_px())`
does *not* wrap, which the mutant satisfies too, and the band assertion is
about the three numbers rather than which one `fit` reads.

Now guarded in both files, at `fit(min_one_row_px())` → must not wrap. Under
the mutant: red on 3 of the 4 screens in the list file (Today has no filters,
so the two widths are equal there and that parametrization is vacuous), and on
Completed and the Scheduler in the SPC file.

The deferral was wrong, and cost a round of review to undo.
