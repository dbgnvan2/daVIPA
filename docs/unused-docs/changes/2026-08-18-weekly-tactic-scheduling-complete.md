Recorded because the pattern is the lesson, not the individual bugs:

- **A hand-written list of tables** referencing `action_items` was wrong twice
  over — one missing table crashed the app at start-up, another was silently
  destroying rows. The list is derived from `PRAGMA foreign_key_list` now.
- **`except Exception` in the transaction manager** did not catch `KeyboardInterrupt`,
  which left the commit gate closed for the life of the process. Every later
  save looked fine and was discarded at close. `./start.sh` runs from a terminal.
- **Two sources of truth for the week boundary** — a cached calendar and a live
  setting — meant changing "First day of week" mid-session lost the next save.
- **Guards that could not fail**: the week-math scan was green on the defect it
  was written for; the wiring guard was satisfied by an import line; a
  `assert ... or True`.
- **Found by running the app, not by the suite**: an item *created* already
  attached to a tactic was never stamped, and picking a tactic by hand was
  silently overridden by the item's own start date.
