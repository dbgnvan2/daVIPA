Plan: `docs/unused-docs/implementation_plan_2026-11-14_plan_week_improvements.md`.
Tests are green on an unmapped screen. Nothing below has been seen on screen.

Open the Plan, choose **Week**.

1. [ ] Drag the divider between the two panes: the seven columns widen and
   narrow together, and none is cut off at the narrowest the pane allows.
2. [ ] Make the window taller and shorter: the boxes grow, and stop shrinking
   at a small size, after which the week scrolls.
3. [ ] An Action Item with a Vision Planning Category, blocked on a day this
   week: its boxes are that Category's colour. Lunch, a Buffer and a Break
   are grey.
4. [ ] Drag an Action Item from the left onto a free box on another day: it
   is blocked there, the week stays up, and the status names the date.
5. [ ] While dragging, the box under the pointer is outlined.
6. [ ] Drop the same item on the same day again: refused, "already in a Time
   Block".
7. [ ] Drop on a purple (meeting) box: refused, nothing written.
8. [ ] Drop on Saturday: asked whether to plan a non-work day; yes writes it.
9. [ ] Drop between boxes or on a heading: nothing written, "Drop on a box to
   plan it".
10. [ ] Click a day: it still opens as a day, and its new block is there.
11. [ ] Each Action Item's Time Block shows its title on its first box, cut
    with "…"; widening the pane shows more of it; the text reads clearly on
    both a pale and a dark Category colour.
12. [ ] Make the window as short as it goes: the boxes stop at 16 px and the
    week scrolls; a title reads whole, and dragging an item over a titled box
    shows the outline on all four sides.
13. [ ] In the week, drag a Time Block from Thursday onto a free box on
    Friday: it moves there, the week stays up, and the status names Friday.
14. [ ] Drag it onto a purple (meeting) box: refused, nothing moves.
15. [ ] Drag it onto Saturday: asked first; yes moves it.
16. [ ] Press on a lunch box and drag: nothing is picked up.
17. [ ] Click a box and a total: the week stays up. Click a date heading: that
    day opens.
18. [ ] Export a day, then move one of its blocks to another day: the status
    says to export the old day again. Do that: the old event is gone from
    Google Calendar. Export the new day: the block's event is there.
