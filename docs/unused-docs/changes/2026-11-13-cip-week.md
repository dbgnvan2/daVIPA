## Walk checklist (on the running app)
1. Plan → Week: seven columns from the selected date; heading "Week Plan".
2. A day with a meeting: purple boxes, "Meetings …" under it; a block: grey.
3. A date never read: grey boxes, "not read"; the line above says "Calendar not read for N of 7 days".
4. Saturday/Sunday (default Day Settings): "no blocks".
5. ↻ Calendar in the week: one read, every column fills in, the line shows the read time.
6. Click a column: that date opens as a day, toggle shows Day. ‹ / › move a week.
7. Drag an Action Item onto the week: nothing changes; the status line says the week is read-only.
8. Narrow window: do seven 64px columns fit, or does the pane scroll sideways? (Not checked; the day pane has no horizontal scroll.)

## Risks / Known gaps
- Column width is fixed at 64px. On a narrow Plan pane the week may be clipped (walk item 8).
- An event that changed on Google after a week read shows only on the next read, as for the day.
