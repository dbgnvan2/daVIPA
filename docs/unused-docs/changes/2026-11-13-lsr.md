## Sweep (2026-11-13): two cold passes, learning-qa and a correctness/UI reviewer
Fixed, each with a guard turned red by mutating the line it names:
- The toolbar wrap never ran: `CTkFrame.bind` binds the frame's inner canvas.
  It is now bound on the toolbar's own Tk frame
  (`test_lsrm10_toolbar_configure_is_bound_on_the_toolbar`).
- Expand/Collapse changed the fixed columns but did not refit; `set_columns`
  now queues a fit (`test_lsrm2_expand_and_collapse_refit`).
- Releasing a divider did not refit, so a drag pushed rows past the list. The
  resizer now calls `on_drag_end` (`test_lsrm5_releasing_a_divider_refits`).
- The fit took screen pixels as CTk units, which breaks at Windows 125/150%
  scaling; it now divides by the widget scaling (`test_lsrm2_fit_uses_unscaled_width`).
- Headers sat 6 px left of their rows: `CTkScrollableFrame` insets its canvas
  by corner radius + border. `ItemListLayout.header_padx` adds that inset.
  No unmapped guard; the mapped `test_lsrm2_resize_event_refits` checks it to 2 px.

Below medium, not fixed (effort budget):
- Fixed columns (Start, Due, Time, chips) do not grow with a larger
  `LIST_ROW_FONT_SIZE`; at 20+ the headings can drift from their cells.
- A refresh during a divider drag destroys the handle, and `dragging` stays
  True until the next drag, so fits are skipped until then.
- A fit still pending when its screen is destroyed prints a Tcl
  "invalid command name" error.
- Hierarchical rows deeper than about 4 levels in a narrow window can be
  indented beyond the title column.
- `title_delta = 34` (badge width + gap) is a literal in three screens.
- `test_lsrm5_drag_shape_survives_resize` saves widths into the session's
  settings file, so later tests start from that shape.
- The same scaled/unscaled mix exists in `ChipTable` and `plan.py`'s fit.

`WeekTacticActionsPanel` subclasses Today, so its list now has four
stretching columns with dividers, not a Title-only divider.

## Risks / Known gaps
- In expanded mode the factor chips (434 px) make the fixed columns wider than
  a narrow window. The shortfall is logged once ("N px narrower than its column
  floors"), and the right-hand columns clip.
- Header/row alignment depends on the per-character estimate (`row_char_px`)
  not under-measuring the font; a title cut too long widens its label.
- A toolbar narrower than its two rows still clips (no minsize, by decision).
